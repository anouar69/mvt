## 📱 What Does mvt Do?

mvt scans your mobile device and looks for indicators that someone might have accessed your phone without permission. It checks:

- **Hidden apps** — Apps that run in the background without your knowledge
- **Unusual activity** — Things happening on your phone that shouldn't be happening
- **Suspicious files** — Files that might be related to tracking or surveillance
- **System changes** — Modifications that could indicate tampering

If you're a journalist, activist, or just someone who cares about privacy, mvt gives you peace of mind by showing you exactly what's happening inside your device.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is mvt really free?
Yes, completely free. No subscriptions, no hidden costs. It's open-source software maintained by security professionals.

### Will this damage my phone?
No. mvt only reads data — it doesn't change anything on your device. Your photos, messages, and apps remain untouched.

### How long does a scan take?
Usually 5-15 minutes, depending on how much data you have on your phone. Larger phones with more apps take longer.

### Do I need to be a tech expert?
No! mvt is designed for regular people. If you can send an email, you can use mvt. The interface is clean and simple.

### What if mvt finds something?
The report explains exactly what was found and what it means. You don't need to interpret anything yourself — it's written in everyday language.

### Can I scan multiple phones?
Yes, connect one phone at a time and run a separate scan for each device.

### Will mvt protect my phone from future attacks?
mvt is a detection tool, not a shield. It tells you if something is already wrong. For prevention, keep your phone updated and only install apps from official stores.

### What should I do after a scan?
If your results are clean, you're good. If there are warnings, back up your important data and consider contacting a professional or doing a factory reset.
